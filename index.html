tog-sim — Vision-Guided High-Speed Pick & Place

A full packaging cell inspired by the Schubert tog.519. A neural network segments unsorted products on a moving belt, a SCARA picks them with a vacuum gripper, and places them into trays that are also moving — the belts never stop. Perception trained entirely on 1,773 synthetic scenes with no manual annotation.
