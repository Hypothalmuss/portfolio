EPSON SCARA · YOLO-SEG · GAZEBO
/ 02Simulation

tog-sim — Vision-Guided High-Speed Pick & Place

A full packaging cell inspired by the Schubert tog.519. A neural network segments unsorted products on a moving belt, a SCARA picks them with a vacuum gripper, and places them into trays that are also moving — the belts never stop. Perception trained entirely on 1,773 synthetic scenes with no manual annotation.

✦ 120/120 cartons placed, no belt stops ✦ 13–14.5 picks/min, 2.7–3.7 mm placement error ✦ YOLO11n-seg at 0.98 mask mAP50, fully synthetic training

UR3e · MOVEIT 2 · ROS 2
/ 03Simulation

UR3e Pick-and-Place Cell

A UR3e with a Robotiq gripper, an overhead camera and a zero-dependency web dashboard. It finds cubes, classifies which are actually reachable, refuses the ones that aren't, and sorts the rest. The emergency stop is the interesting part — cancelling action goals doesn't stop an arm, and finding that out is the whole lesson.

✦ Latching e-stop with 0.0001 rad measured drift ✦ Stable cube IDs that survive occlusion by the arm ✦ ~30 system tests verified against simulator ground truth

Clothoid Path Generation from ArUco Markers

Camera calibration through to trajectory generation: estimate the pose of an ArUco marker from live camera frames, then generate a clothoid curve from the camera's position to the marker.
